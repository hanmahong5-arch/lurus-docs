### Browse and filter

In the Strategy Marketplace, you can filter strategies across multiple dimensions:

| Filter | Description |
|---------|------|
| Market | A-shares (Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges); HK stocks, US stocks, and crypto are planned |
| Strategy type | Trend following, mean reversion, arbitrage, multi-factor |
| Risk level | Conservative, balanced, aggressive |
| Minimum capital | The minimum capital required by the strategy |
| Sort | Return, Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, subscriber count |

### Strategy evaluation metrics

Every listed strategy displays verified quantitative metrics:

| Metric | Good threshold | Description |
|------|---------|------|
| **Annualized return** | &gt; 15% | Annualized compound return rate |
| **Max drawdown** | &lt; 20% | Largest historical loss (peak-to-trough) |
| **Sharpe ratio** | &gt; 1.5 | Excess return per unit of risk |
| **Calmar ratio** | &gt; 1.0 | Annualized return / max drawdown |
| **Win rate** | &gt; 50% | Share of profitable trades |
| **Profit/loss ratio** | &gt; 1.5 | Average profit / average loss |
| **Days running** | &gt; 90 days | How long the strategy has run live |

### Subscribe and deploy

<ol class="lurus-steps">
<li>

Pick a strategy → detail page (includes a **complete backtest report**).

</li>
<li>

Click "**Subscribe**" and confirm the fee.

</li>
<li>

In "**My Strategies**", choose a trading account and set the capital allocation.

</li>
<li>

Click "**Start**" to execute automatically.

</li>
</ol>

**Fees**: some strategies are free, others charge a monthly subscription; subscription fees are deducted from your [Lubei Wallet](/platform/billing#wallet); trading commissions are charged by the brokerage and are unrelated to Lucrum.

### Listing process

<ol class="lurus-steps">
<li>

**Develop locally** the strategy logic.

</li>
<li>

**Validate with backtesting** (at least 2 years of historical data).

</li>
<li>

**Submit for review** (description / risk level / target market).

</li>
<li>

**Platform review** for compliance and risk control (typically 1-3 business days).

</li>
<li>

**Go live**, visible to all users.

</li>
<li>

**Earn income**, settled to your wallet in Lubei.

</li>
</ol>

### Review criteria

| Item | Requirement |
|------|------|
| Backtest data volume | Must cover at least 2 years of historical data |
| Max drawdown | No more than 50% (exceeding this requires an explicit risk note) |
| Risk control measures | Must include stop-loss logic |
| Code quality | No memory leaks, no risk of infinite loops |
| Strategy description | Full explanation of the strategy logic, target market, and risk warnings |

### Revenue share

Subscription revenue generated by a strategy is split as follows:

| Role | Revenue share |
|------|---------|
| Strategy author | **70%** |
| Platform | **30%** |

Income is settled to your wallet in Lubei and can be withdrawn to a bank card.

<div class="lurus-section-head">
  <span class="lurus-section-head__eyebrow"><Icon name="filter" :size="14" /> Strategy types</span>
  <h2 class="lurus-section-head__title">The principles and use cases of four common strategies</h2>
</div>

| Type | Principle | Typical signals/factors | Suited for |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Trend following** | Go with the flow: enter when a trend forms, exit when it ends | Moving-average crossovers, channel breakouts, momentum indicators | One-directional markets (bull/bear); not suited to choppy markets |
| **Mean reversion** | Price reverts after deviating from the mean: buy oversold, sell overbought | RSI, Bollinger Bands, Z-Score | Choppy markets; not suited to one-directional trends |
| **Multi-factor stock selection** | Score stocks across multiple factors and buy the high scorers | PE/PB (valuation), ROE (profitability), 12-month momentum, volatility | Medium-to-long-term holding with low turnover |
| **Pair trading** | When the spread between two highly correlated stocks deviates from the mean, short the outperformer and long the underperformer | — | A low-drawdown, market-neutral, steady strategy |
